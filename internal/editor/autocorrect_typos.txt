# may be more than one word). Lines starting with # are ignored.

# fixes them without asking, so a real word in the left column would get
# "fixed" when someone meant it. Case is handled by the editor: teh and Teh
# become the and The, and ALL-CAPS words (acronyms: TI, IM, ANI) are never
# touched.
#
# Sysops: put your own fixes in configs/autocorrect.txt instead of editing
# this file, which is built into the binary. That one wins, and "word -"
# there turns a fix off.
